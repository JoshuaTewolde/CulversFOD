**Calling all Culver's Ice Cream Lovers...**
Track your favorite Culver's Fresh Frozen Custard Flavor of the Day instantly right from your browser toolbar, with the Culver's FOD Chrome Extension! 

Whether you only care about your regular location, or want to check many nearby Culver's locations at once, this lightweight extension scrapes the flavor of the day directly from Culver's website, saving you time (but not lbs).

Features:
• Quick Location Selector: Add and save your preferred Culver's locations by zip code or city, or by granting location permissions.
• View Flavor of the Day: Instantly view today's flavor along with a salivating photo preview.

That's it! It's as simple an extension as can be.

# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** August 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how our Chrome Extension (the "Extension") handles user information. We take your privacy seriously and are committed to protecting it through strict data minimization. 

### 1. Information We Collect and How We Use It
* **Location Data:** If you grant the Extension permission to access your device location, we temporarily collect your precise geographic coordinates (latitude and longitude). This data is used **exclusively** to query nearby restaurant locations and display their daily menus. 
* **User Preferences:** We save your manually selected restaurant IDs or favorite zip codes locally within your browser using Chrome’s local storage API so your configuration persists across browser sessions.

### 2. Data Retention and Deletion
* **No Server Storage:** We do not transmit, save, or store your location data or preferences on any external database or remote server. 
* **Ephemeral Processing:** Your location coordinates are processed in real-time to execute the location look-up and are immediately discarded. 
* **Local Control:** Your saved restaurant preferences remain on your device. If you uninstall the Extension or clear your browser data, all stored preferences are permanently deleted from your system.

### 3. Data Sharing
* **No Data Selling or Sharing:** We do not sell, trade, rent, or share your personal information or location data with third parties, advertisers, or data brokers.

### 4. Children's Privacy
The Extension does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If we learn that we have inadvertently processed data from a child under 13, we will erase it immediately.

### 5. Compliance with Chrome Web Store Policies
This Extension strictly complies with the Google Chrome Web Store Developer Program Policies, including the User Data Policy and "Limited Use" requirements. 

### 6. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our features. Any updates will be posted directly within this document and the date at the top will be revised.
